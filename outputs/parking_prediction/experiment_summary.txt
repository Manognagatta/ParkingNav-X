PARKINGNAV-X: OCCUPANCY PREDICTION EXPERIMENT

Dataset:
- Total observations: 294
- Parking lots: 5
- Valid 15-minute targets: 204
- Training observations: 142
- Test observations: 62
- Data is synthetic; results are preliminary.

Models evaluated:
- Persistence baseline: MAE 0.1624, RMSE 0.3293
- XGBoost: MAE 0.2471, RMSE 0.5087
- Random Forest: MAE 0.2908, RMSE 0.5313

Conclusion:
The persistence baseline outperformed both machine-learning models
on the current test set. XGBoost performed better than Random Forest.
Overflow observations are sparse and concentrated on one date,
so overflow-specific model results cannot yet be reliably established.

Next steps:
- Improve data coverage and temporal consistency.
- Investigate prediction errors.
- Re-evaluate using a suitable chronological test period.
- Avoid claiming real-world performance until validated on real data.
